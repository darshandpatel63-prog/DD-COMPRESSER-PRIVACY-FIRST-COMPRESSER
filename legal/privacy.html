Privacy Policy

DD Compressor · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Privacy-first: DD Compressor is designed to process selected files locally on the user's device. D.D. Tech Labs does not operate a server that stores uploaded compression files.

1. What happens to your files

  • The selected file is read by the app/browser on your device.
  • Compression is performed locally on the device.
  • The resulting file is returned to you for saving or sharing.
  • DD Compressor does not intentionally upload the selected file or its contents to a D.D. Tech Labs server for compression.
  • Temporary file data may exist in device/browser memory or local storage while an operation is in progress.

2. Accounts and personal information

The core DD Compressor experience does not require an account, password, or user profile. D.D. Tech Labs does not operate a server-side account system for the core compressor.

3. Storage and downloads

When you save or download a compressed result, the file is handed to the browser or Android operating system. Storage after that point is controlled by your device, browser, and operating system. DD Compressor does not maintain server-side storage for your compression files.

4. Network access

Core compression is designed to run locally. The Android app uses network access for Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent features. Network access for these services does not mean that selected compression files are uploaded to a D.D. Tech Labs compression server.

5. Advertising and consent

The Android app integrates Google AdMob and UMP. Depending on ad delivery, device settings, region and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from IP, diagnostics, ad delivery and measurement data, and ad interaction information. The exact data processed can vary by Google's services, device settings, region and consent choices.

The app requests and updates consent information through UMP where required, and provides an in-app route to ad privacy options when available. Ad requests may be limited or unavailable depending on consent status, network connectivity, inventory, account status and Google policies. Selected file contents are not intentionally sent to an advertising provider by the compression engine.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob Help.

6. Analytics and tracking

D.D. Tech Labs does not operate a separate analytics database for the core DD Compressor experience. Google AdMob may process data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, diagnostics, measurement and related advertising purposes as described in Google's documentation and applicable consent settings.

7. Third-party software

DD Compressor includes third-party local processing libraries and Google Mobile Ads/UMP SDKs. Local processing libraries run as part of the application rather than as a D.D. Tech Labs file-upload service. Third-party components may have their own licenses and privacy practices; relevant open-source licenses are provided in the project's source repository.

8. Sharing and sale

  • D.D. Tech Labs does not sell your compression files.
  • We do not intentionally share selected file contents with advertisers.
  • We do not operate server-side storage for your compression files.
  • Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related data as described above and in their applicable policies.

9. Retention and deletion

D.D. Tech Labs does not maintain a server-side retention period for selected compression files because the core compression process is local. Temporary local data is controlled by the device, browser or app. Advertising-related data retention is governed by applicable third-party service policies and settings.

10. Security

Local processing reduces unnecessary transmission of file contents, but no device, browser, software, storage system or network can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and software updated and maintain backups.

11. Children's privacy

DD Compressor is a general-purpose utility. The developer does not knowingly use the app to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising treatment and age-related requirements depend on the app's distribution settings, applicable law and Google's policies.

12. User responsibility

You must have the legal right to process every file you select and must comply with applicable copyright, privacy, confidentiality, licensing and other laws.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application, third-party services, data practices or applicable requirements change.
