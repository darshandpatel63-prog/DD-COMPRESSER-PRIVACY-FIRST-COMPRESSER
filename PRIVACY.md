# Privacy Policy — DD Compressor

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

**Developer:** D D Patel  
**Organization / Project:** D.D. Tech Labs  
**Developer contact:** hello.ddtechlabshub@gmail.com

DD Compressor is designed as a privacy-first file compression tool. Its core compression work is performed locally on the user's device rather than by uploading selected files to a D.D. Tech Labs server.

## 1. What happens to your files

When you select an image, video, audio file, PDF, or other supported file:

- The selected file is read by the app/browser on your device.
- Compression is performed locally on the device.
- The resulting file is returned to you for saving or sharing.
- DD Compressor does not intentionally upload the selected file or its contents to a D.D. Tech Labs server for compression.
- Temporary file data may exist in device/browser memory or local storage while an operation is in progress.

You remain responsible for keeping independent backups of important original files.

## 2. Accounts and personal information

The core DD Compressor experience does not require an account, password, or user profile. D.D. Tech Labs does not operate a server-side account system for the core compressor.

## 3. Storage and downloads

When you save or download a compressed result, the file is handed to the browser or Android operating system. Storage after that point is controlled by your device, browser, and operating system. DD Compressor does not maintain server-side storage for your compression files.

## 4. Network access

Core compression is designed to run locally. The Android app uses network access for Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent features. Network access for these services does not mean that selected compression files are uploaded to a D.D. Tech Labs compression server.

## 5. Advertising and consent

The Android app integrates Google AdMob and UMP. Depending on ad delivery, device settings, region, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from IP, diagnostics, ad delivery and measurement data, and ad interaction information. The exact data processed can vary by Google's services, device settings, region, and the user's consent choices.

The app requests and updates consent information through UMP where required, and provides an in-app route to ad privacy options when available. Ad requests may be limited or unavailable depending on consent status, network connectivity, inventory, account status, and Google policies.

Selected file contents are not intentionally sent to an advertising provider by the compression engine. Advertising-related processing is separate from local compression.

For more information, see Google's policies and documentation: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/

## 6. Analytics and tracking

D.D. Tech Labs does not operate a separate analytics database for the core DD Compressor experience. Google AdMob may process data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, diagnostics, measurement, and related advertising purposes as described in Google's documentation and applicable consent settings.

## 7. Third-party software

DD Compressor includes third-party local processing libraries and Google Mobile Ads/UMP SDKs. Local processing libraries run as part of the application rather than as a D.D. Tech Labs file-upload service. Third-party components may have their own licenses and privacy practices; relevant open-source licenses are provided in the project's source repository.

## 8. Sharing and sale

D.D. Tech Labs does not sell your compression files, does not intentionally share selected file contents with advertisers, and does not operate server-side storage for your compression files. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related data as described above and in their applicable policies.

## 9. Retention and deletion

D.D. Tech Labs does not maintain a server-side retention period for selected compression files because the core compression process is local. Temporary local data is controlled by the device, browser, or app. Users can remove locally saved output through the normal controls of their device or operating system. Advertising-related data retention is governed by the applicable third-party service policies and settings.

## 10. Security

Local processing reduces unnecessary transmission of file contents, but no device, browser, software, storage system, or network can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and software updated and maintain backups of important files.

## 11. Children's privacy

DD Compressor is a general-purpose utility. The developer does not knowingly use the app to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising treatment and age-related requirements depend on the app's distribution settings, applicable law, and Google's policies.

## 12. User responsibility

You must have the legal right to process every file you select. You are responsible for complying with applicable copyright, privacy, confidentiality, licensing, and other laws.

## 13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the application, third-party services, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The **Last updated** date at the top identifies the current revision.
